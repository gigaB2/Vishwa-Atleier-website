Integrated In-House Production Process

1. Yarn & Metallic Zari Integration

Unlike standard weaving houses that rely entirely on outsourced yarns, Vishwa Atelier integrates its sister facility (Lucky Spintex) for in-house metallic Zari covering and Vishwa Fab Weave for precision 2-ply and 3-ply yarn doubling.

2. Computerized Sectional Warping

Up to 14,000 warp ends arranged with micro-millimeter precision to ensure flawless beam density and zero warp-break downtime during high-speed Jacquard weaving.

3. 4-Point ASTM D5430 Inspection

100% of yardage passes through illuminated perch inspection frames under AQL 2.5 acceptance criteria, followed by thermal decatizing, edge shearing, and moisture-sealed export packaging.

4. Private Label & OEM Development

Custom artwork translation into CAD weave structures with rapid sampling swatches and scalable bulk MOQ production (50+ sarees / 1,000+ meters).
